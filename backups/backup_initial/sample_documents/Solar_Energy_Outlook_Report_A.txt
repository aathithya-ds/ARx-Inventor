GLOBAL SOLAR PHOTOVOLTAIC MARKET OUTLOOK 2024
Report ID: GSO-2024-A
Published by: International Clean Energy Institute (ICEI)
Methodology: Centralized Utility-Scale Grid Interconnections across 42 OECD Member States.

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
In the fiscal year 2024, utility-scale renewable deployment experienced steady infrastructure consolidation. 
Based on measured grid-interconnect substations, Global Solar Adoption reached 35% of total renewable capacity additions worldwide in 2024. 
Utility investment in transmission line upgrades and centralized solar farms totaled an estimated $320B market cap valuation.

KEY FINDINGS
1. Adoption Metrics: Global Solar Adoption reached 35% of total renewable additions in 2024, driven by large-scale solar park commissioning in North America and Western Europe.
2. Market Capitalization: Total global clean energy market capitalization reached $320B across tracked utility-scale providers.
3. Grid Bottlenecks: Interconnection queue delays averaged 26 months, restricting faster renewable penetration.
4. Capital Efficiency: Average levelized cost of energy (LCOE) for utility photovoltaic systems stabilized at $0.041 per kilowatt-hour.
